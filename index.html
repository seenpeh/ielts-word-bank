How to paraphrase

Synonyms

Swap words for ones with the same meaning, and check they go together naturally.rose sharply → increased significantly

Word forms

Change a verb into a noun, or an adjective into an adverb.rose significantly → a significant rise

Active ↔ passive

Move the object to the front of the sentence.Experts predict a rise → A rise is predicted

A new subject

Start with a different idea, a time, or the other side of a comparison.Men spent more than women → Women spent less than men

Clause ↔ phrase

Turn a full clause into a noun phrase, or the other way round.because the air is polluted → because of air pollution

Combine two techniques for the best results. Keep numbers, dates and names the same, and never change the meaning.
